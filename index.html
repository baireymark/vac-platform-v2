      Declassified

      Q.01Does VAC represent me before VA?Tap to declassify

      No. VAC is not a law firm or VA-accredited agent. Free accredited help is available from VSOs.

      Q.02Is the calculator an official VA decision?Tap to declassify

      No. It is an estimate. VA assigns final ratings.

      Q.03What should I ask about costs?Tap to declassify

      Ask what is included, which services have fees, and what happens if you stop.

      Q.04Can someone help me contact VAC?Tap to declassify

      Yes. A spouse, caregiver or other helper can reach out with you.

      Q.05What if I was denied before?Tap to declassify

      Start with your decision letter, then look at your options.
